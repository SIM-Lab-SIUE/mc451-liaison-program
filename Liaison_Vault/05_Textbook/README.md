# 📚 Textbook - Course Readings & Notes

This folder contains your **structured notes** from the course textbook and required readings.

## What Goes Here

- ✅ Chapter summaries and key concepts
- ✅ Key quotes and passages
- ✅ Connections to your project
- ✅ Critical analysis of textbook arguments
- ✅ Reading reflections before weekly journals

## Template for Textbook Notes

### Basic Structure
```markdown
# Chapter X: [Chapter Title]

**Reading Date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]

**Relevance to Project:** [One sentence]

---

[Your summary in your own words]

---

## Key Quotes

> "Quote here" (p. XX)

[Why this matters]

---

## My Analysis

[Critical thoughts, questions, connections to project]

---

## Related Notes

- [[Week X Journal]]
- [[Literature Note]]
- [[Project Prospectus]]

**Tags:** #textbook #chapterX #weekX
```

## Tips for Success

### 1. Read Actively
- Highlight key passages
- Write questions in margins
- Note connections to your research

### 2. Take Notes As You Read
Don't wait until after - your brain processes better in real-time.

### 3. Connect to Assignments
Every reading should inform your weekly journals and project work.

### 4. Use for Weekly Journals
Textbook readings often provide material for your weekly reflections.

---

**Pro tip:** Keep textbook notes separate from literature notes. Use this for course-required readings, and 02_Literature for your research-specific sources. 📚
