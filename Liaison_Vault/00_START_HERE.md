# Welcome to your Digital Office

This vault is your **central workspace** bridging Obsidian (note-taking & drafting) and RStudio (analysis & publishing). Use Obsidian for brainstorming, reading notes, and drafting. Use RStudio for data analysis, Quarto rendering, and final submissions.

## Folder Overview

* **00_Inbox**: Quick notes and rough thoughts (Obsidian)
* **01_Journal**: Weekly reading reflections (Obsidian → GitHub)
* **02_Literature**: Reading notes and annotated manuscripts (Obsidian + Zotero)
* **03_Project**: Research workflow from prospectus to final report (Obsidian + RStudio)
* **04_Resources**: Media library and attachments (Obsidian auto-saves here)
* **05_Textbook**: Course reading notes (Obsidian)

## Workflow: Obsidian ↔ RStudio

### When to Use Obsidian:
- ✅ Brainstorming and note-taking
- ✅ Reading annotations and literature reviews
- ✅ Drafting reports and reflections
- ✅ Organizing research materials
- ✅ Linking ideas with `[[WikiLinks]]`

### When to Use RStudio:
- ✅ Data cleaning and analysis (R scripts)
- ✅ Creating visualizations (ggplot2)
- ✅ Rendering Quarto documents to PDF/HTML
- ✅ Statistical testing and modeling
- ✅ Publishing to GitHub Pages

### File Movement Between Tools:
1. **Draft in Obsidian**: Create `.md` or `.qmd` files
2. **Move to RStudio**: Copy `.qmd` files to your R project folder
3. **Render in RStudio**: Use `quarto render` or Render button
4. **Submit**: Upload rendered PDFs to Blackboard

## Task 1: Configure Obsidian Settings

Go to Settings > Files & Links > Default location for new attachments → set to '04_Resources'.
