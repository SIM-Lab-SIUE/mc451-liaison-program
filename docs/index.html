The Five Phases

Our research methods curriculum follows a structured path:

  1. 📰 Phase I: The Journalist (Weeks 1-4) - Weekly reading journals and GitHub setup
  2. 🏗️ Phase II: The Architect (Weeks 2-4) - Project setup, GitHub profile, and archive research
  3. 🔨 Phase III: The Builder (Weeks 5-12) - Develop research questions, codebook, and sampling plan
  4. 📊 Phase IV: The Analyst (Weeks 13-15) - Data wrangling, visualization, and statistical inference
  5. 🎯 Phase V: The Publisher (Weeks 16-17) - Create final web portfolio and professional report

Your Role as a Communication Liaison

As a Communication Liaison, you bridge the gap between:

  • Data Scientists who understand the numbers
  • Creative Teams who understand the audience
  • Project Stakeholders who understand the goals

Your job is to translate, facilitate, and document—turning complex insights into clear action.

The Five-Phase Journey

This program is structured as a progressive learning experience:

Phase Title Focus Duration
🧭 1 Orientation Your role, program structure, workspace setup Week 1-2
🔍 2 Discovery Research methodologies & asking good questions Week 3-4
📖 3 The Dictionary Codebook creation & variable definition Week 5-7
⚙️ 4 Execution Apply methods to real projects Week 8-10
🎯 5 Delivery Present findings & communicate impact Week 11-13

Each phase builds on the previous one. Start with Phase 1 in the sidebar or use the buttons above.
